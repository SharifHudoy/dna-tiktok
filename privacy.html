Privacy Policy

dna is a private tool that publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

What data is used

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in TikTok account (user.info.basic) to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok for the video.upload permission.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored in a private file on the owner's own server and are never shared, sold or published. The app does not collect data about other TikTok users and has no visitors' accounts, analytics or advertising cookies.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the owner disconnects the app. Access can also be revoked in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and websites.
